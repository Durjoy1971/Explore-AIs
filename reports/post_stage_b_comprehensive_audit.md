## Representation-level leakage

`scripts/audit_post_stage_b_model_views.py` checks four experiments, five folds, four views, and seven evaluation sources per unit. There were no exact clean or counterfactual string matches in `raw` or `canon_masked`, and no BanglaPhish OOD matches in any view. The 128 flags are repetitions across configured comparisons. They trace to three distinct Smishing row pairs.

| Boundary | Evaluation row | Equal training input | Affected folds per experiment and brand view |
|---|---|---|---|
| CV and each of the three counterfactual inputs | `cur_43bd66f47b5b` and `cur_c92eaaa1966b` take turns as validation rows | The other row of the pair | 3, 4 |
| SmishDetect held-out validation | `cur_7d47602782d3` | `cur_29c93892c828` | 0, 1, 2, 4 |
| SmishDetect held-out test | `cur_cf8256b4cb6e` | `cur_42a3cdc4a3d8` | 0, 1, 2, 4 |

The audit script compares strings before backbone tokenization. At the configured 128-token cap, all four measured tokenizer revisions each reproduced the 128 collisions across 560 checks covering clean CV, held-out and counterfactual variants. Different revisions and view transformations could introduce additional collisions. It also does not establish semantic uniqueness. No rows, views, or families were removed to obtain a passing result.

## Preprocessing and provenance

- `post_stage_b_data.py:203` builds one vocabulary from the schema defaults, accepted Pilot and batch metadata, and all seven batch registries. The same 364 entries are used for every A–D experiment and every evaluation cohort. The v2 manifest includes vocabulary and registry hashes. NFC and the Bengali/Banglish inflection fixtures ran through the existing schema.
- The A/B/C training row restrictions do not restrict preprocessing inputs. The shared registry is an explicit experiment-wide resource. I recomputed all four clean views from v1 for the 3,999 eligible rows and all three held-out/OOD sets with both the full vocabulary and schema defaults alone. Both variants matched the stored views on every one of those rows. This sensitivity check covers those frozen strings and current rules. It makes no independence claim about future inputs.
- `is_synthetic` distinguishes Stage B additions. The curation provenance classifies the SmishDetect-derived train and held-out source as augmented multilingual, and BanglaPhish as synthetic. “Real-only” denotes the unchanged eligible source cohort. These benchmarks cannot establish effectiveness on natural SMS traffic.
- Adversarial robustness follows raw perturbation, the corresponding view, then model normalization and tokenization. The attack can interfere with entity recognition. Its endpoint is end-to-end robustness of the view pipeline. Counterfactual rewriting retains the existing contact and brand substitutions, which can leave a view unchanged or create a collision. The probe measures score or prediction sensitivity on selected contact-bearing Smishing rows and cannot estimate false positives.
